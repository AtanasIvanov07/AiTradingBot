## Rule (plain words)

Identical to H4, with the parameters fixed. Trade BTC and ETH independently, each in its own sleeve with **50% of
capital**. Each day, at the close:

1. **Trend state:** "on" when `close > SMA(150) × 1.02`, "off" when `close < SMA(150) × 0.98`, otherwise unchanged.
2. **Size:** `size = min(1, 30% / realised_vol)`, with `realised_vol` = std of daily log returns over the last 30 days
   × √365.
3. **Target exposure** of the sleeve = `size` if the trend is on, else **0** (all EUR).

Orders fill at the next open. Size adjustments trade only when exposure must change by more than **10 percentage
points**. Switching the trend off always exits fully.

## Rationale

- Same mechanism as H4: exit in long declines (trend filter), hold less in violent markets (volatility sizing).
- What H4 showed: the drawdown limit is met only with a **slow trend and a low volatility target**. The fast or
  aggressive settings take the first leg of each crash at full size.
- The cost of that: lower returns. In H4's grid this variant made about half the CAGR of the selected one (21.9% vs
  42.0% at 2× costs) and was in the market about half the time.
- **Honest expectation for Gate 2:**
  - Public market history (not our data) says 2023–2024 was mostly a strong rise for BTC and ETH.
  - A strategy that is often half invested, or in cash, usually lags buy & hold in such a period.
  - So **Calmar ≥ 1.2× buy & hold may be hard to reach**, even if the drawdown stays small. A fail there is a
    realistic outcome, and an informative one.

## Success criteria

- **Gate 1 (pre-screen):** the standard pass rule (conventions §7), with the plateau as defined above. That
  includes max drawdown ≤ 40%.
- **Gate 2 (only if Gate 1 passes):** PROJECT_PLAN.md Phase 2, unchanged:
  - OOS Calmar ≥ 1.2× buy & hold, and OOS max drawdown ≤ 35%;
  - positive at 2× costs;
  - parameters ±25% keep ≥ 70% of the Sharpe;
  - beats ≥ 95% of random-entry runs;
  - profitable in ≥ 60% of walk-forward windows;
  - holdout within the Monte Carlo expectations.
- Gate 2 specifics fixed now, before anything is seen:
  - **No re-optimisation in walk-forward.** Parameters stay fixed, so each 6-month window of 2023–2024 is a pure
    out-of-sample test. With 4 windows, "≥ 60%" means at least 3 profitable.
  - **Trade count:** expect well under 50 OOS trades in two years. The Gate 2 result will be labelled
    **low-confidence**, as the plan allows.

## Kill criteria

- Fails the conventions §7 pass rule (with the plateau defined above).
- CAGR ≤ 0 at 2× costs on Binance USDT over 2018–2022.
- Turnover above 400% of capital per year.
- At Gate 2: any Phase 2 kill criterion. Never re-tune to pass (PROJECT_PLAN.md Phase 2).

## Max variants

## Feasibility notes (not part of the test)

- At 30% target vol, exposure while long is often 30–50% of a €50 sleeve, i.e. €15–25 positions.
- 10-point adjustments are €5 trades, the Bitvavo minimum.
- A daily strategy at N=150 trades rarely. Paper trading (Phase 3) would see only a few trades in 8 weeks, which is
  enough to test operations but not the edge.

## Decisions at registration (2026-10-20, approved as written)

1. **Parameters:** fixed at the centre of H4's passing region, knowing that another post-hoc rule would kill it.
2. **Plateau:** H4-grid neighbours plus one new run at 20% target vol.
3. **Gate 2:** fixed parameters (no walk-forward re-optimisation), and acceptance that the result will be
   low-confidence because of the trade count.

## Result / decision

**❌ KILLED at Gate 1 (2026-10-20)**: report [prescreen-H5.md](../reports/prescreen-H5.md), journal #9.

- Fixed variant **n=150, target_vol=30%**. At 2× costs:
  - 21.9% CAGR, −27.3% max drawdown, Sharpe 0.99 and Calmar 0.80;
  - against 50/50 buy & hold at 8.5%, −87.0%, 0.51 and 0.10.
- Passed:
  - criteria 1–5 for the portfolio, BTC (−31.5% DD) and ETH (−30.3% DD);
  - criterion 7 (10.1% CAGR without the best year);
  - the Binance kill check (23.7% CAGR);
  - turnover (276% per year).
- **Failed one criterion, the plateau:**
  - The neighbour n=200/30% has an averaged Calmar of 0.4956, against H5's 0.7090. That's **30.10% lower**; the limit
    is 30%.
  - An independent re-implementation gives 30.11%.
  - This is the neighbour the card flagged as borderline before the run. The rule isn't adjusted.
- The other neighbours pass: n=100/30% (−11%), n=150/40% (+9%), and the new n=150/20% (−3%; 15.1% CAGR, −20.8% DD).
- Interpretation:
  - This is the closest any hypothesis came: one criterion, missed by 0.1 point.
  - But the slow-trend side of the plateau is clearly weaker, and on Bitvavo (2019–2022) the rule made about half of
    buy & hold's return.
  - Under the pre-registered rules it is a kill.
  - As the last allowed hypothesis, it triggers PROJECT_PLAN.md's Phase 1 stop rule. The validation and holdout splits
    were never opened.
